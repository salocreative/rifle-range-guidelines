01Strategy

Our story

Rifle Range is named after the field our vineyard is planted in, on the Hurdcott Estate in Wiltshire. In the First World War this ground was part of Hurdcott Camp, home to Australian soldiers. Today 46,000 vines stand there in bullet-straight rows.

The story in one line

Vines rooted in history.

Why

Made for love of the land

George has the land, the conditions and the story. The question was simple: could we do it? The answer was yes.

The vineyard

9 hectares, 46,000 vines

Planted in 2024 on the Hurdcott Estate, in bullet-straight rows. Every grape is hand-picked.

The wines

Vintage 2025

Bacchus and Chardonnay first, in small numbers. Blanc de Blancs, Pinot Noir and Rosé follow.

The history, in facts we can state

Hurdcott Camp

From late 1914 an army camp ran along the valley. The section on Hurdcott and Naish's farms became Hurdcott Camp.

Australian soldiers

Australians were based at Hurdcott from 1916, with sole use of the camp from 1917.

Command Depots

Hurdcott was home to the AIF's No. 3 Command Depot, and later No. 4. Men trained or regained their fitness there before returning to the front.

The Map of Australia

From 1917, Australian soldiers cut a map of Australia into the chalk of Compton Down. It sits directly above our vineyard today.

A protected landmark

The map was scheduled as a monument in 2001 and restored by volunteers in 2018 to 2019.

The Fovant Badges

Along the valley, regiments cut around 20 badges into Fovant Down, including the Australian Rising Sun.

Sources: Wiltshire OPC · Birtwistle Wiki · Historic England 1020133 · Historic England report 80/2018 · Historic England 1020132 · Fovant Badges Society · Australian War Memorial H16007. Don't add history that hasn't been checked.

UK English

colour, flavour, organise

Write “Rifle Range” in sentences

Always Rifle Range, never The Rifle Range. Capitals only in the logo.

Short sentences

One idea per sentence. Cut any word that doesn’t add something.

“We” and “you”

We planted our first vines in 2024. Not: Rifle Range planted its first vines in 2024.

Active voice

We planted the vines in bullet-straight rows. Not: The vines were planted in bullet-straight rows.

Sentence case headings

Our wines. Not: Our Wines.

Numbers

Use numerals: 9 hectares, 46,000 vines. Spell out a number only at the start of a sentence.

Dates and times

Wine names

The wine, then the vintage: Bacchus, Vintage 2025.

Estate words

Use estate, vineyard and vintage freely. Hurdcott Estate with a capital E, then the estate.

Punctuation

No exclamation marks. Write “and”, not “&”, in sentences.

Taking care

The rules that protect the brand, the estate and the people behind it.

Privacy

Mention the estate freely. Never say where George lives, mention his other estates or name the founders.

Anzac Day and Remembrance

Moments of respect, not marketing. At most a quiet acknowledgement. No products, no offers.

Alcohol marketing

Paid ads target 18+ only. Anyone shown drinking must be, and look, over 25. Wine never brings confidence or social success.

Claims

Only verified facts. No superlatives until there are awards or proof to back them.

Accessibility

Plain English. Alt text on every image, captions on every video. No body copy in capitals.

Trademarks

Rifle Range is registered. Use the marks as supplied, never redrawn.

Claims we don't make yet

  • Largest vineyard in the UK
  • The finest soil in England
  • Best-in-class winemaking
  • Naming the winemakers
  • Unchecked history

The first is an ambition, not a fact. The rest wait for proof, awards or the winery moving in-house.

Clear space and size

The lockup artwork runs edge to edge: exactly as wide as the RIFLE RANGE letters and as tall as the monogram and letters together. Colour

How they're drawn

Grid
32 × 32, with 4px of space inside the edge
Line
0.9 throughout, one even weight
Ends
Square ends, sharp corners
Fill
None. Line only
Colour

Adding an icon

Draw it on the same grid with the same 0.9 line. Keep it simple enough to read at 24px. New icons are added to this set, never taken from another library.

Brand colours only

Heritage green on cream, cream or gold on green. Never a tint, never two colours in one icon.

One set

Only icons from this set. Don’t mix in other libraries or filled icons.

Icons support words

Pair an icon with a label. Only universal icons, such as menu and close, stand alone, and they still need a name for screen readers.

A long arrow, 30 × 12px, 1.3px line, 14px from the label. Gold on green, green on cream

A 2px outline, 3px away, in green on cream or cream on green
One primary per view

One primary button in each section. Everything else is secondary or a text link.

Say where it goes

Short, specific labels: Discover our wines, not Click here.

Never rounded

Square corners, no borders, no shadows.

Website rules

The basics every page follows. Components, image treatments and motion will be added once the brand detail is signed off.

Cream first

Pages start on cream. Green bands and deeper cream tones change the pace.

Square and spacious

Square corners everywhere. Generous margins and space between sections.

Wine first

One headline, one sentence and one button in a hero. The history follows.

Type

Headings in sentence case. Spaced capitals only for the name, labels and buttons.

Real content

Only claims, figures and names Rifle Range have confirmed. Anything else is marked [TBC].

Age gate

Every page checks visitors are of legal drinking age before entry.

Our icons only

Icons from the Rifle Range set, always with a label.

Real photography

Our own estate and wines. AI images only as labelled placeholders.

Readable for everyone

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and controls, visible focus, works with a keyboard.
